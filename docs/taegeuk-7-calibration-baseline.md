The largest errors occur in the repeated tiger-stance and back-stance direction changes. The slow covered-fist sequence is also started late and the following scissor block is split early. Later knee-strike and crossed-block transitions are generally cut 0.3–0.9 seconds early.

The source contains composited front/rear demonstrators from 45.5 to 55.7 seconds. Timing remains reviewable, but that interval is not suitable for single-subject pose-score training. The calibration record therefore uses a 0.65 subject-tracking confidence multiplier, reducing the GPT review weight from 0.6 to 0.39.

This single review is not promoted to a live collective profile. Independent sessions and an expert or additional trusted anchor are still required.
